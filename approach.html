How a study comes together

Four stages, from scoping to delivery

The same disciplined process runs through every engagement, sized to fit the project.

Stage 01

Define the study area

We establish project scope, geography, and the specific effects that matter to your stakeholders.

Stage 02

Build the regional model

A custom input-output model is constructed for the relevant county, region, or state economy.

Stage 03

Quantify the effects

Direct, indirect, and induced impacts are calculated for jobs, earnings, output, and tax revenue.

Stage 04

Deliver & present

A clear, defensible report — and, where needed, direct support presenting the findings.

Methodology

Every dollar spent moves through the local economy three times

Direct effects are the jobs and spending created by the project itself — construction payroll, equipment, materials.

Indirect effects ripple through the supply chain, as local vendors and contractors are paid.

Induced effects come from household spending, as workers spend their earnings at local businesses.
